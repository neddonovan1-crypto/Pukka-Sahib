# Pukka Sahib

A tragicomic district-management game set in a forgotten corner of British
India in the 1920s. You are the District Magistrate. Everyone is watching to
see whether you are a *pukka sahib*. You are not, but the job is to pretend.

> *A "pukka sahib" is the proper, unflappable Englishman who keeps up
> appearances no matter what. The whole game is a very long joke about how
> keeping up appearances turns out to be the entire job.*

## Play the prototype

Open **`index.html`** in any browser. No build step, no dependencies.

Balance five meters — **Revenue, Order, Prestige, Contentment, Composure** —
across 24 fortnights. Every choice that helps one tends to cost another.
Let any of them collapse and your posting ends badly; survive to the end and
your final mix decides which of six endings you get, from a knighthood to
being quietly shipped home "on grounds of health."
